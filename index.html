The greatest equaliser humanity has ever known is the relentless advance of science, shared openly with the world

Full Professor at the Department of Physics, University of Aveiro, and senior researcher at the Associated Laboratory I3N. Working at the intersection of statistical physics, complex systems, and network science to address fundamental challenges in interconnected physical and social structures.


Beyond scientific research, I am deeply engaged in fine arts (SciArt), primarily focusing on oil and pastel painting. My visual work explores the beauty, balance, and emergent complexity that bridge physical principles with aesthetic expression.
