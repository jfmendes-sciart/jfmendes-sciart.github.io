"Complex networks reveal the fundamental principles shaping our connected world and human expression."

Full Professor at the Department of Physics, University of Aveiro, and senior researcher at the Associated Laboratory I3N. My research focuses on Statistical Physics, the study of Complex Systems, and Network Science.


Alongside my scientific research, I actively practice visual arts (SciArt), exploring through oil painting and soft pastels the emergence of textures, light, and patterns found throughout natural and complex structures.

Overview & Highlights

Research Interests

  • Complex Networks
    Evolving topology, multilayer connectivity, percolation, and resilience in interdependent networks.
  • Statistical Physics
    Non-equilibrium phase transitions, critical phenomena, and disordered media.
  • Authored Book
    Evolution of Networks: From Biological Nets to the Internet and WWW (Oxford University Press).

SciArt & Creative Work

  • Oil on Canvas
    Exploration of depth, luminescence, and organic geometry inspired by physical principles.
  • Soft Pastel
    Expressive tonal transitions, texture, and pigments on fine art paper.
  • Convergence
    The interplay between rigorous mathematical structures and spontaneous visual aesthetics.
