Teaching & Academic Courses

Curricular units taught at the Department of Physics (DFis), University of Aveiro, emphasizing fundamental theoretical principles, physical modeling, and the development of analytical problem-solving intuition.

Core Curricular Units (DFis / UA)

Undergraduate · DFis

Fundamentos de Física

Fundamentals of Physics · Department of Physics

Introductory physics curricular unit exploring wave phenomena, thermodynamic processes, fundamental electromagnetism, and atomic/nuclear principles. Focuses on physical foundations and modern technological and environmental applications.

  • Oscilações e Ondas: Movimento harmónico simples, oscilações amortecidas e forçadas, propagação e sobreposição de ondas mecânicas e eletromagnéticas.
  • Temperatura e Processos Térmicos: Temperatura, calor, transferência de energia em processos térmicos (condução, convecção e radiação) e leis fundamentais da termodinâmica.
  • Eletricidade e Magnetismo: Carga elétrica, campo elétrico, potencial eletrostático, circuitos, campo magnético e indução eletromagnética.
  • Energia Nuclear: Estrutura do núcleo atómico, radioatividade, decaimento radioativo, fissão e fusão nucleares, efeitos biológicos e usos da radiação.

Core Physics · UC 15191

Campo Eletromagnético

Electromagnetic Field · Department of Physics

Rigorous analytical treatment of classical electrodynamics using vector calculus. Covers electrostatic and magnetostatic fields in vacuum and matter, boundary value problems, induction, and the complete unified Maxwell equations.

  • Vector analysis in Cartesian, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates
  • Electrostatics: Coulomb's Law, Gauss's Law, and scalar potential
  • Electrostatic fields in dielectrics and boundary conditions
  • Magnetostatics: Biot-Savart, Ampère's Law, and vector potential
  • Magnetic materials, magnetization, and auxiliary field H
  • Electrodynamics: Faraday's induction and Maxwell's equations

Core Physics · UC 2651

Ondas

Waves · Department of Physics

Systematic study of oscillatory and wave mechanics. Focuses on coupled systems, wave equations in mechanical and electromagnetic media, energy transport, boundary reflection/refraction, dispersion, and wave interference.

  • Coupled oscillators and normal modes of vibration
  • Longitudinal elastic waves in gases and solids; transversal waves in strings
  • One-dimensional and three-dimensional wave equations
  • Electromagnetic wave propagation in vacuum and materials
  • Superposition, reflection, refraction, and standing waves
  • Interference, diffraction, and polarization phenomena

Teaching Philosophy

In physics education, mathematical formalism and physical intuition must evolve in tandem. My objective across all levels of instruction is to help students transcend formula memorization and develop independent physical insight: the capacity to look at a complex phenomenon, construct a simplified model, identify the governing symmetries and conservation laws, and critically appraise analytical approximations.

Whether introducing wave propagation, thermal processes, and electromagnetism in Fundamentos de Física, establishing vector calculus rigor in Campo Eletromagnético, or decomposing complex vibrations into normal modes in Ondas, I emphasize physics as a unified and harmonious discipline. Students are encouraged to cultivate analytical discipline, curiosity, and clarity in their scientific communication.
