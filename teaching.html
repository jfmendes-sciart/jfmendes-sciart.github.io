Teaching & Academic Courses

Undergraduate and postgraduate instruction at the Department of Physics, University of Aveiro, bridging mathematical rigor with deep physical insight.

Current & Recent Curricular Units

Undergraduate

Fundamentos de Física

Fundamentals of Physics · DFis / UA

Core introductory physics establishing foundational classical mechanics, conservation principles, translational and rotational dynamics, energy, and thermodynamics for physical sciences and engineering.

  • Newtonian mechanics & conservation laws
  • Linear momentum, angular momentum, and energy
  • Static equilibrium and oscillatory motion
  • Introductory thermal physics and work
Dept. of Physics, UA Lectures & Problem Solving
Core Physics

Campo Eletromagnético

Electromagnetic Field · DFis / UA

In-depth formulation of classical electrodynamics. Exploration of electrostatic and magnetostatic fields in vacuum and matter, electromotive force, Faraday's law, and the complete unified Maxwell equations.

  • Vector calculus in curvilinear coordinates
  • Electrostatics: Gauss's Law, potentials, and dielectrics
  • Magnetostatics: Biot-Savart, Ampère's Law, and magnetic media
  • Electrodynamics & Maxwell's equations formulation
Dept. of Physics, UA Theoretical & Applied
Core Physics

Ondas

Wave Phenomena · DFis / UA

Systematic theoretical study of oscillations and wave mechanics. Covers coupled oscillators, one-dimensional and multi-dimensional wave equations, dispersion relations, superposition, interference, and diffraction.

  • Free, damped, and forced oscillations; resonance
  • Coupled oscillators and normal modes
  • Mechanical and electromagnetic wave equations
  • Wave propagation, reflection, refraction, and diffraction
Dept. of Physics, UA Analytical Physics

Teaching Philosophy

Teaching in physics should go beyond the rote execution of mathematical formulas. My pedagogical philosophy is rooted in developing autonomous physical intuition: empowering students to translate complex observable phenomena into clear analytical models and evaluate the validity of their approximations.

Whether introducing the elementary conservation laws in Fundamentos de Física or guiding students through vector calculus in Campo Eletromagnético and Ondas, I emphasize fundamental principles as a cohesive framework rather than isolated recipes. Students are challenged to cultivate critical thinking, precision in mathematical expression, and curiosity for foundational questions.
